How we measure

A size 40 from 1985 is not a size 40 today.

Each coat is laid flat, closed and unstretched, and measured in centimetres: chest, shoulders, sleeve and back length. Thick fur and shearling take up room inside, so the lining is noted too. Compare with a coat you own, measured the same way.
